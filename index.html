How to Play & Operational Rules

Mode A Bubbles (Letter Recognition)

  • Rising Bubbles: At least five letter bubbles float upward simultaneously.
  • Popping: Press the key on your keyboard matching any visible bubble to pop it and earn points.
  • Targeting Priority: If multiple identical letters are on screen, the bubble closest to the danger boundary pops first.
  • Win Condition: Pop the required target goal (Easy: 30, Medium: 45, Hard: 60) to achieve VICTORY.
  • Game-Over Condition: When escaped bubbles reach the miss limit (Easy: 6, Medium: 5, Hard: 4), the game ends.
  • Penalty: Pressing a key that does not match any active bubble triggers a -5 points penalty, breaks your streak, and shakes the arena.
  • Dynamic Escalation: As your score increases, bubble float speed automatically escalates.

Mode B KeyMagnet (Keyboard Precision)

  • Magnetic Target: The central target display and the on-screen keyboard illuminate the required key.
  • Practice Sets: Choose between Home Row (A, S, D, F, J, K, L, ;), Top Row, Bottom Row, Full Alphabet, or Numbers.
  • Reaction Window: Easy allows 2.6s per key, Medium 1.6s, and Hard 1.0s. Hitting keys within the fast bonus window awards extra points.
  • Win Condition: Successfully complete the target sequence (Easy: 25, Medium: 30, Hard: 35) to finish the drill.
  • Game-Over Condition: Running out of allowed strikes (Easy: 5, Medium: 4, Hard: 3) triggers Game Over.
  • Feedback: Correct keys flash green, wrong keys flash red with distinct audio cues.

🎮 Controls & Accessibility

  • Non-Reloading Controls: Start, Restart, and Reset operate smoothly without refreshing the webpage.
  • Mobile & Touch: Virtual keys and bubbles can be tapped directly on touchscreens.
  • Audio: Toggle procedural sound effects on or off anytime via the header.
